DASHBOARD EXPLANATION

This Tableau dashboard visualizes sales performance using the sales_data_sample.csv dataset (2003–2005). It includes:

1. KPI Cards:
   - Total Sales
   - Total Quantity Sold
   - Average Price

2. Visualizations:
   - Bar Chart: Sales by ProductLine
   - Line Chart: Monthly Sales Trend
   - Filled Map: Sales by Country
   - Treemap: Sales by DealSize

3. Filters:
   - Country
   - ProductLine
   - Year_ID
   (Applied globally to all charts)

Purpose:
To identify product performance patterns, geographical sales distribution, seasonal demand trends, and customer deal size behavior.

The dashboard is fully interactive and provides valuable insights for business decision-making.
