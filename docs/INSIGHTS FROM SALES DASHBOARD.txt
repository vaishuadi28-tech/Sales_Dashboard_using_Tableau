Insights from the Sales Dashboard

1.Overall Performance
The company generated $10M+ in sales with 99K units sold, and the average selling price per unit is $236.

2.Product Performance
Classic Cars is the highest-performing product line, followed by Vintage Cars and Motorcycles.
Trains and Ships show minimal contribution, indicating low demand.

3.Regional Insights
The United States contributes the largest share of sales.
Countries like Canada, France, and Spain also show strong performance, while regions in Asia and Australia have comparatively lower sales.

4.Time-Based Trends
Monthly analysis shows a clear seasonal pattern, with November being the peak sales month.
Sales decline in December after the November peak.

5.Deal Size Distribution
Medium-sized deals dominate the revenue, followed by Small deals.
Large deal sizes account for the smallest portion of sales.
